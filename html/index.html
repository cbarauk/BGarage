After you press Draw, the menu closes and you mark the drop-off area in the world: aim at the ground and press E for each corner (at least 3), then Enter to finish. Last, stand in each spot where vehicles should appear, facing the way they should point, and press E for each (up to 8). Several bays stop people spawning on top of each other. Press Enter to finish. Use Bays on a location later to add or change bays without redrawing the area.
